# Code of Conduct

Be honest, kind, precise, and constructive. Critique claims and implementations,
not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate
falsification of evidence are not accepted in project spaces or when
representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact on the hosting
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or limit
participation to protect a safe and technically honest community.
